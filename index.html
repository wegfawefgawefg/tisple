Tisple ][

Disks built by the Tisple ][ compiler, booting on its Apple IIe emulator compiled to WebAssembly, with its own boot rom (no Apple rom). Tap a game, then tap the screen to start. The on-screen buttons and a keyboard (arrows, return, letters) both work.

Catwiz PlasmaThree sine waves summed for every lo-res block, about 24 fps. LifeConway's Life on the lo-res screen, 40 x 48 cells. SnakeLo-res snake: steer to the orange food, miss the walls. BookA 72 KB book in 80 columns, across main, aux and the language card.
